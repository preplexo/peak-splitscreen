# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First public release.

- Two players in one window: player 1 on keyboard and mouse, player 2 on a gamepad.
- Play Split-Screen sign on the title menu.
- Own camera, HUD, emote wheel and backpack wheel for each player.
- Player 2's passport with a separately saved look.
- Sound for both players through one mix, with one shared soundtrack.
- Weather shown per player: fog, wind, storms, storm sky, lava haze.
- A player who has passed out or died spectates the other one.
